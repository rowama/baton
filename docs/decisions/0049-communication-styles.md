# 0049. Two owner communication styles ship: conversational and operator

## Context

The terse operator style from the owner's developer practice did not suit a layperson owner (§1.1 principle 5).

## Decision

`defaults/communication.md` is conversational (tone and judgment, not a template) and is the default; `defaults/communication-operator.md` keeps the terse operator shape for owners who copy it over `communication.md`.

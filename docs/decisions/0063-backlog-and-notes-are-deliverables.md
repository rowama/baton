# 0063. Baton manages no backlog as a feature, but may write a backlog and notes as deliverables

## Context

§1.3 said "no backlog or idea management (owners use tools of their choice)". In practice that meant the parts of a goal the owner did not choose were lost, and a thought the owner asked the Director to remember had nowhere to go. A backlog is a valid project artifact; the non-goal was too severe.

## Decision

Baton does not manage backlogs or ideas as a feature (no prioritizing, no tracking, no idea store). A backlog and notes are valid project deliverables, and Baton may write them: framing's breakdown (0064) and the owner's notes (0065).

# 0064. Framing publishes its breakdown; unchosen parts can be started later

## Context

Before this, the parts of a goal the owner did not choose were not kept: to do them later, the owner had to describe the goal again.

## Decision

Framing publishes its breakdown as a project document listing every part, its description, and what choosing it starts. Unchosen parts stay there, and a part can be started from it later.

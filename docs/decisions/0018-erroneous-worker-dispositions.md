# 0018. Erroneous worker dispositions: mediation deferred

## Context

**Conditionally deferred risk: erroneous worker dispositions** (discussed 2026-11-02). *Risk:* a worker's `fixed` or `rejected` disposition is wrong but survives (e.g. the confirming review misses it), so a real problem slips through. *Mitigation today:* dispositions are provisional until an independent review confirms them, and persistent disagreement escalates to the owner (§18 #12). *Options discussed and set aside:* (a) the Director judges a dispute itself. The owner's view is that a well-presented dispute needs no subject expertise, the same as a juror or arbiter, but the Director's impartiality must remain sacrosanct, so the Director does not judge. (b) The Director mediates by routing: the runtime opens a pending dispute, and the Director sends it to an independent arbiter worker (a dispatch at `<step>~arbiter`, different vendor from worker and reviewer, returning uphold/reopen with reasons) or escalates it to the owner. The worker and the reviewer must present their evidence so that no subject expertise is needed to decide. This adds a "dispute" concept (§2). *Trigger to revisit:* the owner sees cases where problems slip through because of erroneous worker dispositions. Then reopen this item with these options; (b) is the leading candidate.

## Decision

Deferred, conditionally. Today's mitigation stands: dispositions are provisional until an independent review confirms them, and persistent disagreement escalates to the owner (§18 #12). The Director never judges a dispute. Revisit when the owner sees problems slip through because of erroneous worker dispositions; option (b), routing a dispute to an independent arbiter worker, is the leading candidate.

# 0013. Expert transcripts are kept in full, redacted

## Context

SME transcript retention: full (redacted) or question and answer only.

Suggested: full, redacted.

## Decision

full, redacted: every turn's packet and result are kept under the consultation's `sme/` directory (§12.1).

## Here, now {#here-now}

Use a **quick run** when part of your project needs a document that is good enough to use, not a researched and reviewed one: a lean business plan to start on, a procedure for the shop, a project charter. A helper writes it by following a guide that says what to produce and what good looks like, and you approve it. It is one piece of work, one question to you, and nothing more.

When something deserves more care (research, reviews, several helpers), a full process is the better choice; your assistant can help you decide.

## The guide it follows {#guide}

Every quick run follows one guide. Baton comes with five: a lean business plan, a marketing plan, a standard operating procedure, a project charter and a decision record. Your assistant lists them (`baton guides`) and, when one fits, copies it into your project's `guides/` folder with your say-so. You can read it or adjust it there before the run, and it stays in your project for next time.

## What it reads {#what-it-reads}

Your request in your words, the guide, and any documents you want the result to build on, such as an earlier plan. Your assistant proposes those from the project's documents and you confirm them. It reads nothing else in the project.

## What you will be asked {#what-you-are-asked}

If the guide's questions are not answered by your request or your documents, the helper asks you only what is still missing. Then, once: is the document good enough to use (**Yes, use it**), or **Not yet**, with what to change. Saying **Not yet** has the helper write it again with your note.

## What it produces {#what-it-produces}

The document, in your project's `docs` folder unless your project names another one for documents, under the run's title (for example `docs/business-plan.md`). Once you approve it, it is one of your project's documents, so later work, quick or thorough, can build on it.

## What it needs {#what-it-needs}

One helper that can write.

## The parts {#parts}

| Part | When | You are asked |
|---|---|---|
| Write the document | always | Is this good enough to use? |

It can build on documents your project already has. Before the work that needs one starts, you are shown the document proposed and asked to confirm it.

| Document | Needed | How many |
|---|---|---|
| The guide to follow: what to produce, what good looks like and what to ask the owner | yes | one |
| Documents the owner wants the result to build on, such as an earlier plan | if you have one | one or more |

## If it goes wrong {#if-it-goes-wrong}

- **No guide was given:** the run waits until your assistant confirms one with you.
- **The document is not right:** choose **Not yet** and say what is missing; it is written again with your note. After three tries, your assistant asks you whether to try again or stop.

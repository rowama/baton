## Here, now {#here-now}

Use a **project review** when you want help deciding what to work on next. It is never run unless you ask for it: your assistant offers one when you have chosen several pieces of work from a goal, or when you ask "what should we do next?". Each review is short, and you can have another whenever you like.

A helper reads where the project stands and recommends a few pieces of work to start, each with its reasons, the documents it would build on, and, when the order matters, which should come first and what each waits on. The order is advice: nothing starts until you choose it.

## What it reads {#what-it-reads}

The project's record: every piece of work and where it stands, the project's documents (their names and whether they changed, never their contents), what each piece of work builds on, the pieces left for later in earlier plans, and your project's rules. If you have a written plan or charter, or recorded decisions, your assistant can give them to the review (as you confirm), and its reasons then build on what they say.

## What you will be asked {#what-you-are-asked}

Once: which recommendations to start (**Include these**), or **That's not quite it** if they miss the mark. Your assistant tells you each recommendation with the review's reasons, and adds none of its own. A recommendation that builds on one of your documents says so, and choosing it is also your yes to that document.

## What it produces {#what-it-produces}

A plan of the recommendations, kept in your project as a document: each one with why it is recommended, its place in the suggested order and what it waits on (when the review gives them), what choosing it starts, and where it stands. The pieces you chose start; the rest stay in the plan, and you can start one later.

## What it needs {#what-it-needs}

One helper that can reason and write.

## If it goes wrong {#if-it-goes-wrong}

- **A recommendation cannot start:** because its details do not fit the process, it would write somewhere the project has not set up, or a document it would build on cannot be used. It is reported to you as a gap, never started anyway.
- **The recommendations are not what you need:** choose **That's not quite it** and say why; they are redone.

## Here, now {#here-now}

By default your assistant talks to you like a good project manager talks to a stakeholder: plain language, one topic at a time, best guesses for you to confirm, and short updates. If you would rather have a terse style, say so: "Give me the terse version." It is a setting in your project, so it stays.

## The two styles {#styles}

| | Conversational (the default) | Terse |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | Natural talk, a topic at a time | A short status, then a list of what you must do, then the one decision owed |
| Commands | Explained in words | Each command alone in its own block, ready to paste |
| Best for | Most owners | People who want the least reading |

## Changing it {#changing}

The style is the file `.baton/communication.md` in your project. Baton's terse alternative ships with it; ask your assistant to switch, or replace the file's contents with the terse one. Edit it further to suit you: it is yours, and your assistant follows it.

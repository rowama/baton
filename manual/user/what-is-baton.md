## Here, now {#here-now}

Baton helps AI assistants carry out a larger piece of work reliably, in the right order, with checks, while you stay in charge of the decisions that are yours. You tell your AI assistant what you want, in your own words. Baton keeps the work organised behind it and comes to you only when something needs you.

To start, see [getting started](user.getting-started#here-now).

## Who does what {#who-does-what}

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| **You** | Say what you want, answer questions, and make the decisions that are yours: whether a result looks right, which parts of a goal to start, when to bring an existing project in. |
| **Your assistant** | The AI assistant you chat with, such as Claude Code or Codex. Baton's own documents call it the Director. It keeps the work moving, explains where things stand, and talks to you in your terms. |
| **Helpers** | The models, coding assistants and sometimes people who do the individual jobs. You decide which accounts and subscriptions they may use. See [connecting accounts](user.connect-an-account#here-now) and [choosing models](user.choose-models#here-now). |
| **Baton** | The rule-keeper. It hands each helper exactly what it needs, checks what comes back, and records everything in your project so the work can be picked up again. |

## What you will and will not be asked {#what-you-are-asked}

You are asked only about things that are really yours to decide. Healthy progress is reported briefly, or not at all.

- Whether a result looks right, before the work goes on.
- Which pieces of a bigger goal to start on.
- To confirm a summary of an existing project before it is brought in.
- Answers to a helper's questions, when the work cannot go on without them.

You are not asked to run the machinery, choose a helper for each job, or learn Baton's own vocabulary. Whenever you are asked something, the assistant restates what it understood and waits for you to confirm before anything is recorded. See [answering questions and deciding](user.answer-questions-and-decide#here-now).

## Where the work lives {#where-the-work-lives}

Everything about the work is kept inside your project, in a folder called `.baton`, and committed with the rest of the project. That is why the work can be picked up on another computer or after a long break: see [long-running work and coming back](user.long-running-work#here-now).

Your accounts and keys do not live in the project. They stay on each computer, so they never travel with the repository. See [where API keys are kept](admin.secrets#where-keys-are-kept).

## Purpose

Write down a choice that matters (a supplier, a location, a price, a way of working) together with why it was made, so that people later understand it, do not reopen it without reason, and know what would make it worth revisiting.

## What to produce

One Markdown document with these sections, in this order:

1. **Decision:** the choice, in one sentence, with the date.
2. **Context:** the situation and the need that called for a choice.
3. **Options considered:** each realistic option, with its main advantages and drawbacks, including doing nothing where that was possible.
4. **Why this option:** the reasons, tied to what mattered most.
5. **Consequences:** what follows from the choice, good and bad, including what it rules out.
6. **When to revisit:** the signs or the date that would make the choice worth looking at again.
7. **Who decided:** the person or people, and who was consulted.

## What good looks like

- At least two real options are compared fairly, with the same criteria.
- The reasons would persuade someone who was not there.
- Drawbacks of the chosen option are stated, not hidden.
- Facts are separated from judgments, and any figure has its source.
- It fits on one page.

## What to ask the owner

- What was decided, or needs deciding, in their own words.
- The options they looked at, and anything they ruled out early.
- What mattered most in choosing (cost, time, quality, risk, people).
- What they would want to happen if the choice turns out wrong.
- Who was involved in the decision.

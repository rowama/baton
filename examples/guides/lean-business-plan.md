## Purpose

Help an owner decide whether a small business is worth starting, and how, without a long researched plan. The plan should be good enough to act on and to show a partner or a lender in conversation, not a formal investment document.

## What to produce

One Markdown document with these sections, in this order:

1. **The idea in one paragraph:** what is sold, to whom, and why they would buy it.
2. **Customers:** who they are, how many there plausibly are nearby or online, and what they use today instead.
3. **What is offered:** the product or service, its price, and what makes it the better choice for these customers.
4. **How customers find it:** the two or three channels most likely to work, with a first step for each.
5. **What it takes to start:** equipment, premises, permits or licences, people, and a rough start-up cost.
6. **The numbers:** monthly costs, the price and expected volume, and the month the business would cover its costs. Show the arithmetic.
7. **Risks:** the three that would most hurt the business, and what would be done about each.
8. **First 90 days:** the steps to take, in order, each with who does it.

## What good looks like

- Every number has its basis stated (the owner's figure, a stated assumption, or a source), and assumptions are marked as such.
- The break-even arithmetic adds up and uses the same figures as the rest of the plan.
- Risks are specific to this business, not generic ("a competitor opens nearby", not "market risk").
- Permits and rules are named for the owner to check, never stated as settled law.
- Plain words throughout; an owner with no business training can follow every line.
- It fits on two to four pages.

## What to ask the owner

- What they want to sell, and to whom, in their own words.
- Where the business will operate (town or region, or online), since that sets customers, rules and costs.
- Any figures they already know: prices, rent, equipment quotes, how much they can invest.
- Whether anyone else is involved (partners, staff, suppliers already chosen).
- What would make them decide not to go ahead.

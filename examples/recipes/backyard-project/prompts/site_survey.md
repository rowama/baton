# Survey and photograph the site

Go to where the project will be and record what the layout must respect:

- the usable area in square metres (`area_m2`);
- sun over the day (`full`, `partial` or `shade`);
- the ground: soil, slope, drainage, what is there now;
- how materials get in (`access`);
- hazards: buried services, roots, overhead lines.

Take one photo that shows the whole site and save it as the `site_photo` artifact (JPEG).

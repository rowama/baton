# Check permits and neighbours

Find out whether this structure needs a permit or an association's approval where it
will be built, from the office or rules that decide it (cite them in `basis`). If a
permit is needed, obtain it or record its reference in `permit_ref`. Record whether the
neighbours on affected boundaries were told.

Report only what you confirmed. If you cannot confirm it, set `status` to `blocked` and
say who could.

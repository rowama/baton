# Size levels for backyard projects

Pick the **lowest** level whose description fits. When in doubt between two, pick the higher one and say why.

| Level | Typical work | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| `minor` | Planting or refreshing a flower bed, a few pots, mulching, a small border edging. An afternoon or a weekend; no structure; no digging deeper than a spade. | Materials, then the work, then the owner's acceptance. |
| `standard` | A raised bed, a gravel path, a small patio of loose pavers, a trellis. Several days; changes the layout of the yard; may need hired tools. | A site survey with photos and a layout plan before materials. |
| `major` | A shed, a pergola, a deck, a retaining wall, anything with a foundation, anything near a property line, or anything a permit or the neighbours may care about. | The owner approves the layout; permits and neighbours are checked; the finished structure is inspected. |

Signals for `major`: footings or a slab; height over 2 m; roofed; electrical or water connections; within 1.5 m of a boundary; a homeowners' association.

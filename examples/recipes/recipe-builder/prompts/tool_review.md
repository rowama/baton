# Review the tool against its contract

You did not build this program. Check it, in the working tree, against its contract
(`tools/<id>/contract.json`, the id being `tool.tool`) and the `rubric`. The examples have passed
already; your job is what passing examples cannot show. Report findings only.

For each finding give a short `title`, the `location` (a file and line), the `detail` and a
`suggested_fix`. Set `coverage.complete` to false, with a note, for anything you could not judge.
